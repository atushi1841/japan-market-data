Japan Price Drop Alerts

The value of a price alert is entirely in its latency. An item that drops below your threshold today is actionable; the same information next week is a footnote, because someone else has already bought it.

Because each run returns records keyed on the listing URL, a scheduled scrape plus a single comparison gives you an alert that fires the day the price moves. No browser automation on your side, no fragile selectors to maintain.

FAQ

How do I avoid alert spam?
Alert only on a drop larger than a set percentage, and suppress repeats by storing the last-alerted price per URL.
Can I watch several marketplaces at once?
Yes — run the relevant actors on the same schedule and merge their records before diffing.
What about items that reappear?
Keep history keyed on URL. If a URL reappears after ending, note the gap rather than treating it as a continuous price series.
