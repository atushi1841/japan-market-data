Collecting DLsite Pricing & Listing Data

Programmatic access to Japan's largest doujin marketplace for market research and price tracking.

DLsite (dlsite.com) is the biggest marketplace for Japanese doujin — voice works, ASMR, games, and art, mostly adult (R18 / RJ-numbered) titles. If you build tools for the doujin niche — a recommendation engine, a price tracker, a sale watcher, or a catalog — the first thing you need is a reliable way to pull structured listing and pricing data at scale.

Why raw DLsite scraping is painful

DLsite serves heavy JavaScript, splits shops by language (and separately handles the R18 home vs general maniax trees), and paginates everything. A hand-rolled crawler breaks whenever the front-end changes. And because much of the catalog is adult content, you want a data source that returns clean fields without needing you to drive a browser session yourself.

Recommended: the DLsite Scraper returns 60+ fields per title — RJ number, title, circle/author, genre tags, price, rating, sales count where available, cover URL, and release date. Runs on the Apify cloud and is billed per item.

Typical use cases

  • Sale-watch automation — DLsite runs frequent sales; compare current price vs base price and alert when a wishlisted RJ/PRE title drops.
  • Market research — analyze genre and price distribution across the catalog for creators deciding what to price their next work.
  • Wishlist & cataloging tools — build your own database of titles with stable RJ numbers, independent of DLsite's search which returns noisy results.

Keep it legal and scoped

This kind of data collection is for your own analysis and tools. Don't republish the actual works (that's copyright), and don't expose any buyer/circle personal data. Title metadata, genres, and prices are fine; the purchased content is not.
