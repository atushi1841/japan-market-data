Collecting Japanese Used Car Data for Importers

goo-net is one of Japan's largest used-car portals. Here's how to pull its inventory and price statistics into your sourcing workflow.

If you import Japanese used cars — to Africa, Central Asia, Oceania, Caribbean or Latin America — the edge isn't finding a car, it's finding the right price on the right stock quickly. goo-net (goo-net.com) lists nationwide inventory from thousands of dealers. Automating collection of that data gives you:

  • Continuous sourcing feed matched to your markets (e.g. 4x4s, kei trucks, specific models)
  • Price statistics to negotiate and set your landed-cost expectations
  • Stock volume trends for a model — is it scarce or oversupplied right now?

What to collect per listing

make / model / grade
year & mileage
price in JPY
dealer & location
auction vs stockboard status
listing URL & photos
scraped_at timestamp
Recommended: the goo-net Japan Used Cars scraper pulls nationwide inventory to a JSON dataset and can also return model-level price statistics. Billed per result; a targeted keyword sweep is cheap enough to run daily.

From raw feed to sourcing signal

The value is in the delta. Keep your scraped copy locally each day and filter for: price below your threshold, low mileage, model in your export range, and new listings you haven't seen before. That turns a big crawl into a short, actionable watchlist.

Compliance note

Use listing/price data for sourcing decisions only. Don't republish dealer contact details or individual seller info en masse — keep the dataset to vehicle + price + location, which is all you need.
