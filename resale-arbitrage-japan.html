Resale Arbitrage Japan

Arbitrage between Japan and Western markets has a long history in collectibles, cameras and cars, and it survives because the information is genuinely hard to assemble. Prices are published in Japanese, on marketplaces with different catalogue conventions, and the interesting spread is often invisible from a search result page.

Systematic screening requires structured data on both sides. The Japanese side is what these scrapers provide: prices in yen with condition, images and URLs, in a format you can join to your own comparison table.

FAQ

Does this include shipping or duties?
No. Those are your own costs and must be added before treating a spread as profit.
How do I avoid false positives?
Compare condition and edition, not just titles. Reissues and box damage are the usual causes of a spread that looks too generous.
Is this suitable for fixed-price or auction sourcing?
Both. Auctions give realised prices, C2C marketplaces give quick-turnover asks.
