Anime Figure Price Data

Figure prices are volatile in a way that is hard to see from a single storefront. A scale figure that sat at retail for a year can double once a rerun window closes, and the market often splits by grade: sealed, opened-displayed, and box-damaged move at very different prices.

Collecting prices from Japanese second-hand and auction sources side by side is what makes that spread visible. The scrapers return graded listings with yen prices, so you can group by condition instead of comparing sealed against opened.

Use cases

  • Detect rerun-driven price cliffs by sampling the same search weekly.
  • Decide whether an opened copy at a discount is genuinely cheaper than a sealed one.
  • Build an internal price guide for a shop that buys and resells figures.

FAQ

Do prices include the Japanese consumption tax?
Prices are recorded as published by the source. Treat them as the listed number and handle tax rules in your own pipeline.
Can I track a specific series only?
Yes — a run is scoped by the search terms you provide, so you can follow one series, one maker, or one character.
How often should I re-run?
For volatile collectibles a weekly sample is usually enough to catch a rerun effect; daily is only worth it if you are actively trading.
