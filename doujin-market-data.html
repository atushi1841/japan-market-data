Doujin Market Data

The doujin market is a genuine independent publishing economy, and unlike most indie scenes its catalogue is centralised enough to measure. Genres shift fast, a single popular release can define a season, and pricing follows a campaign rhythm rather than a fixed retail model.

Having the catalogue in structured form turns those impressions into observations: how many works a tag is gaining, what a genre's typical price band looks like, and which circles publish repeatedly.

Use cases

  • Genre analysis: measure how listing volume concentrates across tags over time.
  • Publisher tracking: identify circles that release consistently within a niche.
  • Marketplace features: power a category browser or recommendation list with fresh data.

FAQ

Is this legal to use commercially?
The scraper returns catalogue metadata — titles, prices, genres, URLs. Whether a downstream use is permissible depends on your own product and the source's terms; the data itself is product metadata, not creative content.
How deep does a sweep go?
Depth is set by the search scope you configure, from a narrow tag to a broad category sample.
Can I join this with external sales data?
The output is plain JSON with stable field names, so it merges easily with your own tables.
